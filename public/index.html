FAQ

Common questions

Does Jessica sound like a real person?
Yes. She uses natural-sounding AI voice and is trained to speak in a warm, professional tone. Most callers don't realize they're speaking with AI unless they directly ask.
How long does setup take?
Most clients are live within 24 hours. You fill out the onboarding form, we configure and test Jessica, then you forward your number. That's it.
What happens if Jessica doesn't know the answer?
She'll politely acknowledge the limit of her knowledge, offer to take a message, and — if it sounds urgent — transfer the call to your team immediately.
Can I update her knowledge base later?
Absolutely. Email or message us anytime with changes — updated hours, new services, new FAQs. We process updates within 24 hours.
Does this work with my existing phone number?
Yes. You set up call forwarding from your existing number to your dedicated Jessica line. Callers always see your number.
What industries do you support?
Any business that takes inbound calls: medical/dental, legal, real estate, home services, beauty, automotive, fitness, and more.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. Month-to-month. Cancel any time from your dashboard, no questions asked.
